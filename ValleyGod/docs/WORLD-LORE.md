# World lore lock

This is **our** solar system. This is the **Milky Way**. People live on the planet that will later be called **Earth**.

Do not invent a fantasy planet, a second Earth, a made-up sun, or a private cosmology.

## Miniature Earth

This slice is not a full-size planet. It is a compact layout: eight continents with ocean between them. The sculpted valley is Willow basin. The other seven lands are placeholder discs so the camps, claims, and camera can be played. Leftover ground on each continent starts unclaimed. Tribes grow a claim by building, and they do not cover the whole land.

The god camera can fly the whole miniature Earth (Shift to move faster, **G** to hop to the next continent). Villagers still never know a watcher exists.

## People

Eight tribes, one on each continent. Each tribe has one teacher and learners who pick up that teacher’s skill over time. Starter characters are adults, 21 or older.

Pairing is adults-only. This slice does not place child characters. When two adults of the same tribe stay fed and near each other, a new adult can join that camp. There is no intimacy scene.

## Same sky, later

The other planets and the named stars exist. They are not content in this slice. No planet travel, no solar-system map, no naming Mars or the Milky Way in the game. Optional flavor may notice the night sky the way a person on the ground would: dark, lights, the white disc. That is all.

Not in this slice:

- Space
- Leaving Earth for orbit or another world
- A named cosmos, star charts, or planet list
- Invented worlds or moons of fiction
- Child characters, or pairing for anyone under 21
- Nudity or intimacy scenes
