# World lore lock

This is **our** solar system. This is the **Milky Way**. The villagers live on the planet that will later be called **Earth**.

Do not invent a fantasy planet, a second Earth, a made-up sun, or a private cosmology.

## Same sky, later

The other planets and the named stars exist. They are not content in this slice. No planet travel, no solar-system map, no naming Mars or the Milky Way in the game. Optional flavor may notice the night sky the way a person in a valley would: dark, lights, the white disc. That is all.

## This first slice

One stone-age valley on Earth. Dirt, river, trees, shelters, weather, a compressed day. The player is an invisible watcher. The valley does not know a god exists.

**Population:** eight adults (four women, four men) are every human alive on Earth. No other tribes or background people. Children come later, not here.

Not in this slice:

- Space
- Leaving the valley for orbit or another world
- A named cosmos, star charts, or planet list
- Invented worlds, moons of fiction, or “another planet like Earth”
- Extra humans, other villages, or child characters

If a later slice looks up, it looks up at **this** sky.
